Boxley Collector

Privacy Policy — Last updated September 2026

Boxley Collector ("the app") is developed as an independent, single-developer project. This policy explains what data is and isn't collected when you play.

Data we collect directly

None. The app does not require an account or login, and does not collect or store any personal information on our own servers. Your game progress (collection, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information — Apple's own privacy policy governs that data.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch them for in-game rewards) using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, and this may be used for ad personalization depending on your App Tracking Transparency choice on iOS. You can review Google's practices in their own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children's privacy

Boxley Collector is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll address it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
