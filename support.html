Common questions

I made a purchase but didn't get my item/coins.
Try Restore Purchases from the Shop screen first. If that doesn't fix it, email us with your purchase receipt and we'll sort it out.

An ad didn't play but I got the reward anyway.
This can happen when no ad is available to show — the game grants the reward regardless so you're never blocked from playing. It's expected behavior, not a bug.

I lost my progress.
Progress is stored on your device. Reinstalling the app or switching devices without restoring purchases first can affect saved progress. Email us with details and we'll see what we can do.
